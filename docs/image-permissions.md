## How to confirm

1. Find the holder: the archive, publication, organization, or photographer.
2. Check its terms of use, or ask for permission in writing. Keep the email or a link to the terms.
3. Record who confirmed it and when in the table above, and note any credit wording the holder requires.
4. Update the credit in `scripts/site-photos.json` (and so `data/stops.json`) to match, and set `"permission": "confirmed"`.
5. Add a line to `PROCESS_LOG.md`.

If permission can't be obtained, remove the image from `data/stops.json` (and its file from `images/`) before the site is shared publicly.

See also: [content-guidelines.md](content-guidelines.md#images-and-rights).
